Update of Pistol or Handgun Defenses Against Bears
Ammoland Inc. Posted on March 16, 2022 by Dean Weingarten
Source: https://www.ammoland.com/2022/03/update-of-pistol-or-handgun-defenses-against-bears/

1935 Combination .45-70 and 9mm or .30 Luger pistol on Unuk River Alaska by Jack Johnstone
Jack Johnstone was armed with a cut-down 45-70 and a Luger pistol. A huge grizzly charged across the Unuk River. Jack emptied his rifle into the animal, then emptied the Luger magazine before the current swept the bear away.

Spring, 1974, Washington State, .357 magnum and snare, black bear
Professional bear hunter Ralph Flowers was charged at close quarters by a snared black bear. He fired four rounds from a .357 magnum revolver into the bear's head.

October 1981 Cold Bay Alaska, .44 mag, .375 rifle, .375 pistol, Grizzly bear
Larry Kelly and partner were charged by a brown bear at a beach shack. Kelly fired a .44 Magnum at three feet, then additional shots as the bear turned. Finished with a T/C .375 JDJ.

22 September 2005, Cow Call Grizzly Attack, Crossbow and .44 Magnum
A guide was charged by a sow grizzly with two cubs. After being thrown down, the hunter shot the bear in the chest with a crossbow. The guide then shot the still-breathing bear in the head with a .44 magnum.

14 August 2010, WY: Fish Creek Bear Attack: Savage 99 .308 and Taurus Tracker .41
A grizzly charged from about 15 feet. Lead hunter fired a Savage 99 .308 at 8-10 feet; partner opened up with a .41 magnum. Both emptied their firearms.

October 19, 2010 .44 Mag and 12 gauge at the North Fork of North Crandall, Park County, Grizzly Bear
Guide fired one .44 magnum shot as a sow came from nine feet; wrangler fired a 12 gauge with 00 buck almost simultaneously. The bear dropped and rolled downslope.

October 8, 2012 Grizzly bear Pinyon Ridge of Pinedale, WY Combination 6mm and .44 mag
Two wolf hunters were charged from about 40 yards. They shot twice with a 6mm rifle and three times with a .44 mag pistol. The dead bear was later found.

September 9, 2014 Combination .40 caliber and bow, Grizzly Shedhorn Ridge Montana
Bowhunter hit a charging sow in the snout with an arrow, then fired about five shots from a .40 Taurus 24/7 as it fled. On a second charge he emptied the magazine.

September 10, 2015 Pass Creek Grizzly .340 Weatherby Mag and 10mm 30 miles SW of Cody, Wyoming
A bear on a sheep carcass charged from about 10 yards. Shot with a .340 magnum then four times with a 10mm Glock. Charge stopped.

Combination 13 May 2021, Grizzly .338 ultra mag and .454 Casull Alaska
Hunter hit a charging grizzly with a .338 ultra mag, then finished the charge with a .454 Casull revolver at close range, including shots at 5 yards.

Spring of 2020, Combination bow and pistol, XDS pistol, .45 ACP Black Bear
Archery hunter arrowed a black bear twice. When it charged he dropped the bow and shot it with a .45 ACP XDS at 7 feet. The bear dropped at 5 feet.
