Handguns Proven 98% Effective in 205 Documented Bear Attacks
Ammoland Inc. Posted on October 22, 2025 by Dean Weingarten
Source: https://www.ammoland.com/2025/10/handguns-proven-98-effective-in-205-documented-bear-attacks/

August 19, 2020 | Becharof Wildlife Refuge, Alaska | Grizzly | .45 Magnum
Anglers at Featherly Creek. A sow charged; Stumpf fired a round past a partner, over the bear's head at less than 15 yards. The bear whirled and sprinted into the brush. Same sow later charged a guide and clients.

March 28, 2024 | Greenwood Lake, New York | Black bear | Unknown handgun
A large black bear broke into a home on Seventh Road. The owner, alerted by his dog, shot the bear once in the midsection with a legally owned handgun after it crawled through a window.

April 25, 2024 | Great Falls, Montana | Grizzly | Unknown handgun
A shed hunter northwest of Wolf Creek was charged by a grizzly at about 20 yards. He fired five shots from about 30 feet to 10 feet, grazing then killing the bear. He was not injured and was not carrying bear spray.

July 19, 2024 | Columbia Falls, Montana | Grizzly | Unknown handgun
A 72-year-old man picking huckleberries about 2 miles north of Columbia Falls was charged and attacked. He shot and killed an adult female grizzly with a handgun and was hospitalized. FWP called it a surprise defensive encounter.

July 30, 2024 | Marion County, Florida | Black Bear | 9mm
Jesse Tittle's dogs encountered a bear. While trying to retrieve a puppy he fired a warning shot with a 9mm but struck the bear. The animal tried to climb a tree and fell.

August 20, 2024 | Resurrection Trail, Alaska | Brown bear | 10mm
Tyler was charged by a roaring grizzly. He cleared a 10mm just before the bear hit him, firing while falling backward. The bullet grazed his own quadriceps and may have hit the bear.

September 2, 2024 | Island Park Caribou-Targhee National Forest, Idaho | Grizzly | Unknown handguns
An archery hunter was knocked down and bitten by an adult male grizzly west of Henrys Lake. Both men used sidearms to shoot and kill the bear. The injured hunter had non-life-threatening injuries.

September 6, 2024 | Siren, Wisconsin | Black Bear | Combination Rifle & 10mm
Ryan Beierman fired eight 10mm shots at a wounded black bear from 6-10 feet and missed; the bear mauled his leg, arm, and face. His son Owen then shot the bear with a rifle, stopping the attack.

September 13, 2024 | Tepee Creek, Montana | Grizzly bear | Unknown handguns
Bowhunters north of West Yellowstone were charged by a sow grizzly with two yearling cubs. They shot and killed the sow with handguns and injured one yearling. Hunters were not injured.

September 13, 2024 | Montana | Grizzly bear | 10mm Glock 20
A sow woofed then charged at 20-30 mph. Barkley Mercado fired about a half-dozen 10mm shots; dirt kicked around the bear. She skidded, stopped, and ran back toward cubs. Father nearly used bear spray.

September 26, 2024 | Upper Green River Basin, Wyoming | Grizzly | 10mm Glock
A sow with cubs charged a hunter backed against a rock at about 10 yards. After the bear bit his leg his pistol jammed; he cleared it and continued. About 10 shots, at least four hits including a fatal head shot.

October 3, 2024 | Meeteetse, Wyoming | Grizzly bear | 10mm Glock
Kalkowski had just chambered a round when a bear burst from timber. He fired immediately; the bear folded and rolled to his feet. He emptied the rest of a 15-round magazine. Incident lasted seconds.

October 5, 2024 | Hidden Lakes, Gallatin County, Montana | Grizzly | Unknown handgun
A hunter south of Hidden Lakes was charged by a sow with two cubs. He shot the bear with a pistol then climbed a tree. FWP dispatched the wounded grizzly. Hunter uninjured.

May 21, 2025 | Choteau, Montana | Grizzly | 10mm and .45 ACP
At 30 yards a sow put her ears back and charged. Justin (Glock 20 10mm, three shots) and John (1911 .45 ACP, five shots) fired. The bear veered off wounded around 20 yards. Variable wind made spray impractical.

June 24, 2025 | Ft. Walton Beach, Florida | Black bear | Unknown pistol
Robinson watched an unusually bold bear, yelled from a cracked door, then shot it 12 times when it walked toward him after knocking over a griddle. The ~180 lb male dropped after walking a few dozen feet.

September 29, 2025 | Big Sky, Montana | Grizzly | Unknown handguns
Two hunters near a known elk carcass yelled at a grizzly. The bear came toward them; each fired once with a handgun. Both the bear and the hunters left without further incident.
